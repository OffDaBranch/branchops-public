# Public Roadmap 2026

## Objective

Provide a public-safe roadmap that explains where BranchOps is going without exposing internal implementation details.

## 2026 Roadmap Themes

### Phase 1 — control foundation
- strengthen entity and asset visibility
- improve workflow execution reliability
- tighten audit-friendly record behavior
- formalize repo and product boundaries

### Phase 2 — intake and workflow maturity
- move public-facing inquiries into owned intake paths
- improve normalization of incoming operational actions
- expand first-party workflow coverage for repeated business actions

### Phase 3 — operator visibility
- expand dashboard coverage for controlled records
- improve reporting surfaces for assets, licensing, and workflow state
- add more public-safe product proof for external understanding

### Phase 4 — productization readiness
- keep the private platform hardening while improving public-safe product narrative
- expand demo-ready materials, screenshots, and diagrams
- support cleaner external conversations around capability and positioning

## Public Constraints

This roadmap is directional. It does not disclose private backlog items, secrets, or exploit-relevant implementation details.

## Optimization

A public-safe roadmap helps communicate maturity and direction while preserving the privacy and strategic value of the internal platform repo.
